Privacy Policy for नशा मुक्ति साँप-सीढ़ी खेल
(Addiction Recovery Snakes & Ladders Game)

Effective Date: December 2024
Last Updated: December 2024

Introduction

Welcome to नशा मुक्ति साँप-सीढ़ी खेल (Addiction Recovery Snakes & Ladders Game), developed by जिला प्रशासन श्रीगंगानगर (District Administration Sri Ganganagar) and Raman Kumar Asija. This privacy policy explains how we handle information when you use our educational game application.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Player Name: When you choose to enter your name for personalized gameplay
  • Game Preferences: Your selected game piece color and number of players
  • Audio Settings: Your music and sound effect preferences

Automatically Collected Information

  • Game Statistics: Number of moves made, game duration, snakes encountered and ladders used, questions answered during gameplay, game completion status
  • Device Information: Basic device compatibility information for optimal game performance

Future Data Collection (Planned Updates)

In future updates, we may collect additional information as provided by Google Play Store services:

  • App Usage Analytics: Total screen time and session duration
  • Download Statistics: App installation and update information
  • Performance Metrics: Game performance and crash reports
  • User Engagement: Feature usage and interaction patterns
  • Aggregated Data: Anonymous usage statistics for app improvement

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely to:

  • Provide personalized gameplay experience
  • Track game progress and statistics
  • Remember your preferences during the current session
  • Improve game performance and user experience

Data Storage and Security

  • Local Storage: All personal data is stored locally on your device only
  • No Cloud Storage: We do not store any personal information on external servers
  • Session-Based: Player names and preferences are only retained during active gameplay sessions
  • No Account Creation: The game does not require account creation or login

Data Sharing and Third Parties

  • No Current Data Sharing: We do not currently share, sell, or rent any personal information to third parties
  • Google Play Services: We may use Google Play Services for app analytics and performance monitoring in future updates
  • Advertising Policy:
    • Dynamic Ad Implementation: We reserve the right to add or remove advertisements at any time without prior notice to users
    • Google Ad Networks: If ads are implemented, they will be served through Google's advertising networks (Google AdMob, Google Ad Manager)
    • Ad Personalization: Google may use your device information and usage patterns to show relevant ads
    • Ad Data: Google's advertising services may collect data as per their own privacy policies
  • Asset Loading: Game assets (images, sounds) may be loaded from GitHub repositories for game functionality, but no personal data is transmitted
  • Analytics Services: Future updates may include Google Analytics or Google Play Console analytics for app improvement

Children's Privacy

This educational game is designed to be suitable for all ages, including children. We are committed to protecting children's privacy:

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children
  • Player names are optional and stored locally only
  • No online interactions or chat features are available
  • The game operates in a safe, offline environment

Animal and Creature Imagery Disclaimer

Important Notice Regarding Game Imagery:

  • Educational Purpose Only: All images of snakes, animals, or creatures used in this game are purely for educational and gaming purposes
  • No Harm Intended: This game does not promote, encourage, or condone harm to any animals, creatures, or species
  • Fictional Representation: Snake and ladder imagery represents metaphorical concepts of challenges and progress in addiction recovery
  • Conservation Awareness: We respect wildlife and support animal conservation efforts
  • Cultural Sensitivity: All imagery is used respectfully and in accordance with cultural traditions of board gaming
  • No Real Animal Content: No real animals were involved in the creation of this game
